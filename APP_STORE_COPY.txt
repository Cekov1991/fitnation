DESCRIPTION — App Store & Google Play  (4000 char max)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

YOUR GOAL. YOUR SCHEDULE. YOUR PROGRAM.

Fit Nation builds you a real training program — the kind a coach would write —
then tracks every set and tells you exactly when you've earned the next weight
jump.

A PROGRAM BUILT AROUND YOU
Tell us your goal, your experience, and how many days you can train. Fit Nation
builds a personalized 5-week program — the right split, the right exercises,
the right rep ranges. Want a fresh approach? Regenerate it in one tap.

LOG LIKE THE NATION DOES
• Live sessions with set-by-set logging (weight, reps, rest)
• Smart prefill from your last performance — no more guessing your starting
  weight
• Built-in rest timer and progression cues
• Personal record alerts the moment you hit them

PROGRESSION THAT ACTUALLY PROGRESSES
Double progression for weighted lifts. Total-reps progression for bodyweight,
TRX, and band work. Fit Nation tells you when you've earned a weight increase —
no more random number-picking, no more stalled programs.

INSTANT WORKOUT GENERATOR
Off your plan today, or short on time? Choose your focus (Push, Pull, Legs,
Upper, Lower, or Full Body), set your duration, and Fit Nation builds a session
in seconds. Don't like it? Regenerate. Tweak. Then train.

BUILD YOUR OWN
• Create unlimited custom routines and programs
• Build plans, workouts, and templates from scratch
• Reorder exercises, set targets, swap moves on the fly

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
• Strength score with percentile ranking against lifters with your profile
• Strength balance across 17 muscle groups
• Weekly volume, time-under-the-bar, and workout count trends
• Per-exercise charts: max weight, best set reps, total volume — all over time
• Calendar view of every session you've ever logged

THE EXERCISE LIBRARY
Every exercise comes with HD video, muscle-group diagrams, and a clear
description — tagged by the equipment it needs, so you always know what fits
your setup.

WHO TRAINS WITH THE NATION
• Lifters who want a real structured plan, not another random workout list
• Beginners who want to walk in knowing exactly what to do today
• Busy people who need training that fits their schedule, not the other way
  around
• Advanced athletes who want clean data and real, earned progression

Your goal — fat loss, muscle gain, raw strength, or general fitness — it
doesn't matter. Fit Nation builds the program around you, tracks every rep, and
pulls you forward with the rest of us.

Download Fit Nation. Start your program today. Move with the Nation.

BRAND VOICE REFERENCE  (System 3 — The Movement)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Core vocabulary:
  Nation / The Nation   → refer to the user community
  Movement              → refer to the app/brand itself
  Train with us         → CTA framing
  Rise with us          → aspirational close
  We move together      → solidarity hook
  Pull in the same direction → progression metaphor

Avoid:
  Hardcoded color names (always use ThemeContext in code)
  "AI" — the workout generator is algorithmic, not AI
  "Medical-grade" or prescriptive health claims
  Gym-partnership framing ("official app of...") — the US listing is
    direct-to-consumer; partner gyms are a separate channel, not the pitch
  Claims that the app knows the user's gym equipment — per-user equipment
    selection isn't wired into onboarding yet (punch list item 14)
